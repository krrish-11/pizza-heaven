About The Pizza Heaven

The Pizza Heaven is a local favorite tucked into Sector 19, Kharghar — the kind of place regulars walk to without checking the menu first. Whether you're grabbing a quick bite between errands or settling in for a proper meal, we keep it fresh, fast, and full of flavor.
